iBridge Traditional Chinese heading font

Source: Google Fonts, Noto Serif TC variable font, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notoseriftc
License: NotoSerifTC-OFL.txt (included unchanged).

ibridge-serif-tc.woff2 is a self-hosted WOFF2 subset containing characters used in
this release's Astro/TypeScript/CSS source and Latin characters U+0020–U+00FF.
Weight range: 200–900 in the original; website requests 400–700.
Font loading uses font-display: swap. New characters fall back to Songti TC,
Noto Serif TC or PMingLiU. No third-party font request is made by visitors.
For extensive new Chinese copy, regenerate the subset or supply the full font.
English headings use Georgia with Times New Roman as a system fallback.
